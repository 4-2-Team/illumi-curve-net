Method, in brief

IllumiCurveNet is a zero-reference enhancer — it needs no paired low/normal-light ground truth. It learns 8 iterative curve maps plus an adaptive gamma from six unsupervised losses computed only from its own input/output:

L_color — penalizes RGB channel-mean divergence
L_spa — preserves local spatial contrast structure
L_exp — pulls patch brightness toward a 0.6 target
L_TV — smooths the predicted curve maps
L_contrast — pulls local contrast toward an ideal band
L_texture — matches Sobel-gradient texture pre/post

Because these images are already well-exposed NASA SVS crescent-moon renders — not genuine low-light captures — this evaluation measures domain transfer to a mostly-black-sky, low-detail scene, not restoration against ground truth. Quality is judged with three no-reference IQA metrics (NIQE, BRISQUE, PIQE — lower is better on all three) alongside the model's own training losses, computed identically for the pretrained baseline and the fine-tuned run over the full 318-image test split.

Findings & caveats

  • ✓The pretrained baseline already generalizes well to this out-of-domain astrophotography set: enhancement drops mean NIQE from 5.37→3.45 and BRISQUE from 42.4→23.6 across all 318 test images, with no fine-tuning at all.
  • ✓Fine-tuning on the 2,534-image crescent training split for 100 epochs cuts the model's own weighted training loss by 14.6% on held-out test data, driven almost entirely by L_color (−98.5%) and L_TV (−77.5%) — the model learns these near-monochrome, high-contrast frames trivially satisfy color balance and curve-smoothness objectives.
  • •L_exp stays the largest single term even after fine-tuning (0.142, only −17.8%): most of a crescent frame is pure black sky, so pulling the patch-mean brightness toward the 0.6 target used for terrestrial low-light photos is structurally difficult here. That target was tuned for a different domain and is a candidate to revisit for lunar imagery specifically.
  • ✗Fine-tuning is a mixed result on perceptual quality: BRISQUE improves further (23.6→22.1, −6.3%), but PIQE regresses 15.3% (20.4→23.5) and L_spa / L_texture both rise slightly (+9.7% / +5.6%) — consistent with the model suppressing sky-background texture/noise in a way PIQE penalizes even as it looks visually cleaner.
  • •No early stopping triggered in 100 epochs (val loss was still slowly decreasing) — there is likely headroom for a longer run or a lower learning rate before overfitting becomes the binding constraint.
